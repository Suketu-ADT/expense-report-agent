# Automated Evaluation Report

Generated by `backend/app/services/evaluation_service.py` (run it directly —
`python -m app.services.evaluation_service` from `backend/`, with `PYTHONPATH=.`
— to reproduce these numbers against the current code).

This is the hard-numbers counterpart to the live demos in `demo_guide.md`:
instead of watching one run succeed, it scores the pipeline against labelled
data and synthetic trials.

## Categorization accuracy

Source: `backend/data/evaluation/categorization_labels.csv` (26 hand-labelled
merchants).

| Tier | Correct | Total | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rule** (deterministic keyword rules, always offline) | 16 | 16 | **100%** |
| **Fallback** (requires the LLM fallback; needs `GROQ_API_KEY`) | 10 | 10 | **100%** (with a key configured) |

The rule-tier accuracy is asserted at 100% in CI (`tests/test_evaluation.py`)
and never depends on network access. The fallback tier is reported, not
asserted, since its score depends on an optional API key — the system is
designed to keep working (falling back to "Other / Uncategorized") even
when that key is absent.

## What this does and doesn't claim

This harness measures the pipeline's own internal consistency and labelled
accuracy on these fixtures — it is not a claim that 100% of real-world
expense data would categorize or reconcile perfectly. The rule-tier and
review-agent numbers are deterministic and reproducible offline; the
fallback-tier and large-dataset numbers depend on the categorization
fixture and dataset chosen, and are reported alongside that context rather
than as a universal accuracy claim.
